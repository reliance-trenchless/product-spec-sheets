# Reliance Trenchless — Product Spec Sheets

Reliance Trenchless Technology (RTT) brings together three specialist manufacturers to supply point-repair materials and equipment for trenchless pipe rehabilitation through one point of contact. The range covers pipe plugs, repair packers, Instant-Lock sleeves, internal seals, fiberglass resin and mortar sprayers for trenchless pipe repair.

## Product range

Grouped by the five product families used on the website. Size ranges are as published on each product page.

### [Trenchless Point Repair Systems](https://reliancetrenchless.com/products/point-repair-systems)

- [Instant-Lock Internal Pipe Repair Sleeve](https://reliancetrenchless.com/products/instant-lock-sleeves-small) — mechanical point repair sleeve in stainless steel and EPDM, DN150–600. Not CIPP lining or an internal joint seal.
- [Large-Diameter Mechanical Internal Pipe Repair Sleeve](https://reliancetrenchless.com/products/instant-lock-sleeves-large) — Instant-Lock Large, DN600–2000, stainless steel and EPDM, installed with the SL Big Tool.
- [Pipe Rehabilitation Packers](https://reliancetrenchless.com/products/repair-packers) — standard, bypass, long lateral and wheeled models, DN50–1500.
- [Fiberglass and Resin](https://reliancetrenchless.com/products/fiberglass-resin-repair-system) — resin and reinforcement for local patch repairs, DN100–1000, used with a matching repair packer.

### [Internal Joint Sealing](https://reliancetrenchless.com/products/internal-joint-sealing)

- [Internal Pipe Joint Seal](https://reliancetrenchless.com/products/internal-pipe-seal) — seals a joint between host-pipe sections from inside the pipe; WW/DW series, DN150–4000.
- [Internal Pipe Joint Mechanical End Seal](https://reliancetrenchless.com/products/internal-pipe-end-seal) — seals the ends of CIPP liners and pipe sections from inside, using an EPDM sleeve and stainless steel expansion bands.

### [Inflatable Pipe Plugs and Testing](https://reliancetrenchless.com/products/pipe-plugs-and-testing)

- [Multi-Size Inflatable Pipe Plugs](https://reliancetrenchless.com/products/multi-size-pipe-plugs) — one plug covers a diameter range, DN50–1500; MS isolation and MSBP bypass models with separate inflation and back-pressure limits.
- [Single Size Pipe Plugs](https://reliancetrenchless.com/products/single-size-pipe-plugs) — inflatable stoppers for one nominal size, SS 300 to SS 4000.
- [Testing Plugs and Steam Plugs](https://reliancetrenchless.com/products/testing-plugs) — TP-DN150 to TP-DN1000 test plugs in 4 bar and 6 bar series; not a blocking or bypass plug.

### [Pipe Defect Pretreatment](https://reliancetrenchless.com/products/pipe-defect-pretreatment)

- [Reliance Point-Repair Jack](https://reliancetrenchless.com/products/point-repair-jack) — prepares pipe defects before a localized repair, DN150–600. Confirm pipe material before ordering.

### [Mortar Rehabilitation](https://reliancetrenchless.com/products/mortar-rehabilitation)

- [Manhole Mortar Sprayer System](https://reliancetrenchless.com/products/mortar-sprayer-system) — mortar pumps and spraying equipment for manhole and pipeline rehabilitation, DN600–1800.

Not sure which family fits your pipe? See [Applications](https://reliancetrenchless.com/applications), which matches pipe condition and repair task to each family.

## How to request a quote

Send your enquiry through the [contact page](https://reliancetrenchless.com/contact) and include:

- Measured inner diameter at the defect (and the nominal DN if that is all the drawing shows)
- Pipe material and defect type, or the intended task
- The medium in the pipe
- Working conditions, such as pressure or the inflation and back pressure a plug must hold
- Product or model, and quantity
- Destination country or port, and target date
- Whether the order is urgent ([air freight](https://reliancetrenchless.com/about#air-freight)) or sea freight is acceptable — air freight is optional
